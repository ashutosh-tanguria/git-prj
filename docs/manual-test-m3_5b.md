# M3.5b Office Quick view

Local automated tests use mocked GitHub reads and real renderers on ports 4173/4174. No real provider or production deployment is claimed. M4 remains unstarted.

1. Deploy the app and sandbox on different registrable sites as described in sandbox/README.md. Verify the exact app origin in frame-ancestors, COOP/COEP, no shared cookies, and the app's exact sandbox frame allowlist. Never paste credentials into origin B.
2. In a disposable repository, open fixtures/office-reading.docx. Confirm the text, table and image. Use Quick Look and the full viewer, Download, Copy path and Open in provider. Test corrupt DOCX and a file above 50 MB. Legacy DOC/PPT and ODF must show metadata, Download and the unsupported message.
3. Open fixtures/office-reading.xlsx. Summary has a saved formula result of 6. Select Notes with mouse, Arrow keys, Home and End. Test a sheet above 5,000 rows and 256 columns: notice visible, at most the first 5,000 rows/256 columns available. Scroll to the end and switch back. Saved formula results are displayed without recalculation.
4. Test a genuinely encrypted XLSX and a damaged archive. Expect the password-protected or damaged notice plus Download. No password entry or decryption is offered.
5. Open fixtures/office-reading.pptx. Confirm two slides and their images, the fidelity notice, page/slide navigation and zoom from 25 to 400 percent. Test j/k, PageDown/PageUp, +/-, 0 and ? with focus both inside and outside the frame. Focus Render in cloud: tooltip says "Available in a later release"; activating it sends nothing.
6. Open fixtures/office-hostile.docx. Its JavaScript hyperlink and scripted HTML altChunk must remain inert. The app-owned untrusted notice remains outside the frame. Inspect requests and Window/MessagePort payloads: only a random channel, path/size, bytes, renderer results and bounded controls cross the boundary. No provider authorization, app storage or cookies may reach origin B.
7. Repeat in English and Hindi, light/dark, 360px and 200 percent zoom. Test keyboard focus, sheet tabs and Download with a screen reader. Confirm the toolbar wraps without horizontal page overflow. Review the three refreshed Styleguide baselines; independent Hindi review is still required.
8. Repeat on Firefox, WebKit and a representative Android device, including a large workbook and repeated open/close. Record browser/device, rendering differences, time and memory. Production cookie/header and physical-device performance gates remain manual.

Quick view intentionally omits advanced Office features. Fonts and pagination may differ. The conservative sanitizer removes SVG slide shapes, forms, active links and embedded HTML; the document cannot run scripts. The spreadsheet parser runs in a worker terminated after 20 seconds. The app ends an unresponsive viewer after 25 seconds. Closing or changing the file closes the private port and destroys its frame. No Office file bytes are persisted by the sandbox.
